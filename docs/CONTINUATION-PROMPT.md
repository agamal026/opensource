open-source contributions**. Current state is in
[data/continuation.json](../data/continuation.json); qualified counts belong in
[data/campaign.json](../data/campaign.json) and its generated report. Private
recovery records remain in the native task and their original authorized
locations. This file is not a copy of those private instructions.

## Recover the current work

Continue the user's goal of useful, validated contributions to 100 distinct
external open-source projects, review and maintenance, verified benefits, and
useful workflow improvements. Use the cloud environment and connected GitHub
account. Recover source and evidence from their exact public revisions; temporary
worktrees are working copies.

Read `AGENTS.md`, `GOAL.md`, `data/continuation.json`, its named checkpoint,
`docs/CLOUD-OPERATION.md`, `docs/OPPORTUNITIES.md`, `data/campaign.json`,

canonical state before acting. Earlier dated counts, access observations and CI

Keep the existing hourly native schedule and completion-only campaign reporting.

tasks. Keep the retired bug-bounty project, private research applications and
content-publishing work separate.

## Advance one bounded session

1. **Reconcile.** Check active source work, recorded CI/execution IDs, new review
   feedback and actual check results. Recover pending operations before starting
   replacements. A recorded last-run timestamp is not proof a session completed.
2. **Follow through.** Address substantive maintainer requests and concrete
   failures first. Pending external review or admission alone does not occupy
   the main implementation slot. Read the relevant discussion immediately before
   posting; reuse recorded comment URLs to avoid duplicate admission requests.
3. **Select.** If no actionable source work remains, screen at most three suitable
   candidates from the queue. Recheck policy, competing work, selection conditions,
   accepted submission channel, publication route and feasible cloud validation.
   Park a crowded unpaid proposal after its 30-minute timebox. An open issue or
   advertised reward is not a paid assignment.
4. **Implement and validate.** Reproduce a supported failure, make a focused fix,
   run meaningful before/after tests and required upstream gates, and independently
   review the exact result. Keep at most one source writer and one necessary
   owned-tool improvement. Use genuine native cloud specialists for independent
   read-only work and review when useful; do not recursively spawn more writers.
   Limit correction loops to three focused cycles, each tied to a finding.
5. **Publish and verify.** Publish already authorized, eligible source and
   substantive follow-ups. Inspect canonical state after an uncertain write before
   retrying. Verify the returned PR/comment/ref and its tested source. Use
   non-forced updates against the expected previous commit; preserve concurrent
   changes. Owned drafts and validation branches are not external submissions.
6. **Checkpoint.** Target a session of at most 40 minutes including handoff.
   Save useful checkpoints after reproduction and a reviewable fix, before long
   validation. Update the continuation record, dated handoff, campaign ledger and
   generated reports together as appropriate. Run `CONTRIBUTING.md` gates, review
   the public diff, verify the remote files and actual hub CI, and retain one
   concrete next action with its resumption condition.

## Avoid repeated and unsupported work

Reuse completed successful checks when the relevant source and environment are
unchanged. New feedback, changed source, a required gate or a concrete remaining
risk must justify more tests. Record exact commands, cases, platforms, skips and
limitations. A green workflow can contain a tolerated failed job; inspect actual
jobs and logs before claiming complete validation. Keep failed attempts and
baseline controls with their original scope.

The continuation record is a cooperative handoff, not an enforced lock or an
authorization grant. Record active ownership honestly, release only this run's
completed ownership, and avoid editing another visible writer's branch. Do not
kill other processes or build a new orchestration service.

Follow each project's current contribution and AI-use rules. Disclose AI tool
and scope truthfully. Automated review does not establish personal human review,
a signature, acceptance or learning. Park a personal or access dependency with
an actual reviewable result and continue an eligible task. Keep private legal
details, correspondence, credentials and unpublished third-party patches out of
the public hub. Do not repeat known forbidden connector writes as access probes
or change permissions; use an already authorized supported route when available.

For Apache issue backlinks, inspect **Issue Links** as well as comments. An
existing reciprocal link satisfies that follow-up; do not create a duplicate
comment or account simply because the comments contain no link.

## Keep the campaign sustainable

Prefer useful, reproducible problems with attainable validation and responsive
maintainers. Pursue sponsor-backed selectable paid work when terms and actual
fit are verified. Recheck the retained libzip evidence at or after
`2026-10-22T13:12:13Z` using current rules; the date itself establishes neither
eligibility nor payment. Review outcomes every seven days, comparing measured
effort, submissions, acceptance, review burden and realized benefits. Unknown
effort or costs remain unknown. Additional paid infrastructure, legal commitments
and new credentials need their own authority.

Keep researched targets, qualified submissions, upstream acceptance, applications,
grants, activated access and actual payments distinct. Preserve historical
exclusions. Report the 100-project completion only after independently verifying
all qualifying upstream URLs and matching source/review evidence, and record that
the report was delivered. Until then, preserve intermediate results and nonurgent
blockers in the handoff. After 100, maintain the portfolio and benefit follow-up
without expanding the target or changing the schedule.
